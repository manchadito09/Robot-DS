Floor 4 Guide
Online

1 · Wi-Fi (do this first)

  • The robot must be connected to Wi-Fi.
  • Your phone and the robot must be on the same Wi-Fi network — otherwise this page will not work.
  • Recommended network: DirectSupplyGuest (connect both the robot and your phone to it).

2 · Start the robot (on the robot's screen - On the desktop in files)

Plug a mouse into the robot for these steps — you need to right-click.

  1. Right-click Navigation — and select run as Program. Wait about a minute.
  2. Right-click Robot Web QR and select run as Program. Scan it with your phone to open this page.

3 · Tell the robot where it is

  1. Go to Map and press I'm here.
  2. Tap where the robot is, then tap the way it is facing. Done — no RViz.

4 · Use it

  • Take me somewhere — tap a place; the robot leads you and tells you about it.
  • Talk to me — tap the mic and speak in English, or type. Ask questions ("what is the glass room?"), ask to be taken somewhere, or say "show me around" for a tour.
  • Tour — tick the stops in the order you want, then Start tour.
  • 🔊 Speaker (top-left) — tap to silence the robot, tap again to give it its voice back. It keeps saying everything either way; muted, you read it here instead. The button turns red while it is muted, because a silent robot looks exactly like a working one.
  • Map — Go / Add a point / Remove / I'm here. Pinch or use + − to zoom.
  • Drive me — steer it by hand. Camera — see its view.

5 · Faces — the robot greets people by name

  • Add me — teach it your face. Type your name, press Start, and it shows what it sees and talks you through it — turn your head as it asks (left, right) so it knows you from any angle. Next time it says hello by name. You can also upload photos instead of scanning.
  • Say hello to people I know — the switch that turns greetings on or off. It is remembered through reboots.
  • It only says hello. The robot's own microphone is far-field and sits next to its speaker, which never became reliable — so to ask it anything, use Talk to me here, where your phone's mic does the listening. The greetings say so out loud.
  • Greet the same person again after — 30 sec / 1 min / 5 min / 10 min: how long before it re-greets someone. Use 30 sec for a demo, 5 min for every day.
  • Remove someone — tap their name in the list and confirm.
  • Privacy — it stores a fingerprint (a set of numbers), never a photo, and adding yourself is voluntary.

6 · Good to know

  • The red STOP button (bottom-left) stops the robot at any time — anyone can press it.
  • Only one phone controls the robot at a time; the others can watch.
  • The robot needs a moment to think before it answers or sets off.
